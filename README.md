### Spotlight 1: [Swiggy Delivery Transparency — One-Screen Redesign](./Swiggy-Delivery-Transparency-One-Screen-Redesign)

> **"Making Delivery Detours Understandable: A One-Screen Redesign of Food-Delivery Order Tracking"**

- **The Problem:** When delivery partners take apparent detours due to batched order routing, customers experience anxiety without contextual explanations, driving unnecessary "Where is my order?" (WISMO) support queries.
- **The Solution:** A high-clarity, one-screen enhancement introducing:
  1. Contextual ETA adjustment badges (*"Updated (+4 min for stop)"*)
  2. Anonymous delivery sequence timelines
  3. Expandable *"Why this route?"* progressive disclosure pills that protect user privacy
- **Primary Metric:** Delivery-status clarity score (1–5 scale target: $\ge 4.2$) & reduction in status-check support tickets.
- **Artifacts Included:** Problem framing, user discovery ($n=5$), baseline vs. redesign visual mockups, edge states & risk analysis, instrumentation & rollout decision rules.

### Spotlight 2: [Campus Ride-Sharing & Travel Reliability — User Research & Discovery](./Campus-Ride-Sharing-User-Research)

> **"Decoding Off-Campus Mobility: Reliability, Trust, and Informal Coordination Breakdown"**

- **The Problem:** Students departing campus for fixed-time deadlines (trains, flights, weekend home trips) face severe cab cancellations and rely on 300+ message WhatsApp groups that collapse into coordination entropy, flakes, and no-shows.
- **The Discovery ($n=5$):** Uncovered that reliability at high-stakes deadlines and verified institutional trust (hostel/year) are the true prerequisites for sharing rides—cost savings are secondary to punctuality and safety.
- **The Solution Direction:** "Campus Pool Hub"—a verified micro-carpooling concept with university SSO, destination departure slots (Airport/Station/City), and structured commitment locks.
- **Primary Metric:** Match Completion & Departure Confidence Rate ($\ge 80\%$) and zero missed-departure tolerance.
- **Artifacts Included:** 9-point cross-participant synthesis matrix, behavioral pattern breakdown, hypothesis validation evaluation, HMW opportunity mapping, system sequence flow, and UI architecture mockups.

### Spotlight 3: [Beauty E-Commerce Competitor Analysis — Nykaa vs. Purplle vs. Tira](./Beauty-Ecommerce-Competitor-Analysis)

> **"How Three Beauty Platforms Convert, Retain, and Monetise Different Segments of the Indian Beauty Shopper"**

- **The Strategic Focus:** Comparative PM teardown across 6 core operational dimensions (target user, feature architecture, loyalty/membership mechanics, shipping unit economics, optimizations, and shortcomings).
- **The Platform Archetypes:** 
  1. **Nykaa:** Retention via catalogue scale, content-to-commerce, and spend-gated loyalty (*Nykaa Prive*).
  2. **Purplle:** High-velocity discount engine and monetized paid memberships (*Elite / ElitePro*) to de-risk COD.
  3. **Tira:** Premium luxury discovery, AI beauty tech, RUSH delivery, and omnichannel retail across 32 flagship stores.
- **The Product Opportunity:** *"The New-User Conversion Gap"*—identifying how spend thresholds and paywalls suppress top-of-funnel conversion for single-item baskets, and specifying a PRD for a **Zero-Threshold Discovery Trial Pass** with unit economics defense.
- **Artifacts Included:** Market positioning quadrant chart, 6-dimension comparative matrix, system sequence diagrams, feature PRD, and execution Gantt chart with decision rules.

### Spotlight 4: [Instagram Metrics Breakdown & Measurement Framework](./Instagram-Metrics-Breakdown-Framework)

> **"Deconstructing Engagement: Moving from Passive 'Time Spent' to Meaningful Social Connection"**

- **The Strategic Problem:** Optimizing for passive time spent or video views leads to "zombie scrolling"—driving high screen time while user satisfaction and durable retention erode.
- **The North Star Metric:** **Weekly Active Connectors (WAC)**—measuring unique users who perform $\ge 1$ high-intent social interaction (DM, comment, story reply, or outbound share) within a 7-day window.
- **The 3 Supporting Drivers:** 
  1. **Creation Rate:** Ensures supply liquidity of authentic peer updates.
  2. **Share Rate:** Measures content resonance and curational intent via DMs.
  3. **Week-4 Retention:** Validates durable habit formation for newly onboarded cohorts.
- **The Non-Negotiable Guardrail:** **Negative-Experience Rate (NER)** (hides, blocks, reports per 1,000 sessions) to prevent algorithms from optimizing for outrage, flame-wars, or spam.
- **Artifacts Included:** Metric hierarchy flywheel diagram, analytical evaluation of interaction weighting, and real-world A/B test decision scenarios.

### Spotlight 5: [Nykaa First-Order Beauty Trial Pass — One-Page PRD](./Nykaa-First-Order-Trial-Pass-PRD)

> **"Eliminating Checkout Friction for Small-Basket New User Acquisition & Repeat Conversion"**

- **The Problem:** Nykaa's standard ₹70 shipping charge on orders $< ₹299 creates prohibitive friction for first-time shoppers wanting to make a small trial purchase (e.g., an ₹199 lipstick), causing high checkout abandonment.
- **The Feature Solution:** An automated, zero-coupon welcome benefit that waives the ₹70 shipping fee on a new user's first order under ₹299 and unlocks an optional branded sample mini.
- **Fraud & Abuse Protection:** 4-layer defense model (hardware device fingerprinting, carrier-verified OTP, delivery address clustering, and payment instrument limits).
- **Success & Guardrail Metrics:** First-order conversion lift target of $+10\text{--}15\%$, $\ge 25\%$ 60-day repeat order velocity, bounded by logistics cost caps ($\le ₹65$) and RTO baselines.
- **Artifacts Included:** End-to-end functional requirements matrix, low-fidelity checkout wireframes, system sequence diagrams, and phased A/B rollout schedule.
